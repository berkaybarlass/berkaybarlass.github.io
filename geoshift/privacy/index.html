Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 October 2026

GeoShift is an Android app that lets you simulate your device location for testing, privacy and development.

Data GeoShift stores

Everything below is stored only on your device and is never sent to us:

  • Places you save as favorites and your teleport history (coordinates, place names, addresses).
  • Routes you create (waypoints, speed and loop settings).
  • App settings (units, simulation options, map style, last map position).

You can delete favorites, history and routes in the app at any time. Uninstalling the app removes all of it. If Android backup is enabled on your device, this data may be included in your personal Google backup.

Location

GeoShift asks for location permission so it can run a location-type foreground service while a mock session is active and show your real position on the map. Your real location is not stored, logged or transmitted by GeoShift.

Place search

Searching for a place or showing an address uses Android's built-in geocoder. On devices with Google Play services, the search text or coordinates are processed by Google to return results. See the Google Privacy Policy.

Maps

Maps are rendered on your device with MapLibre using vector tiles from OpenFreeMap (map data © OpenStreetMap contributors). Loading tiles sends standard web requests (your IP address and the map area being viewed) to the tile server; GeoShift adds no identifiers.

Advertising

GeoShift shows ads through Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and use the advertising ID, device information, approximate location derived from IP address, and ad interaction data to show and measure ads. Where required (for example in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland), GeoShift asks for your consent through Google's User Messaging Platform before personalized ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Ad privacy choices, and you can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings.

See How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Analytics

GeoShift contains no analytics or tracking SDKs of its own.

Children

GeoShift is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

We will update this page when the policy changes. The date at the top shows the latest revision.
